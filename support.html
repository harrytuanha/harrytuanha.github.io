Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Email harry.tuan.ha@gmail.com with the game name, your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

I bought something but don’t have it. Open the game → Shop or Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I stop personalised ads? Deny tracking in the iOS prompt or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA/UK use the game’s Settings → Privacy choices.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Hỗ trợ

Gửi email tới harry.tuan.ha@gmail.com kèm tên game, đời máy và phiên bản iOS. Mua rồi mà không thấy: vào game → Cửa hàng hoặc Cài đặt → Khôi phục giao dịch (cùng Apple ID). Hoàn tiền do Apple xử lý tại reportaproblem.apple.com.
