Mana Orbit

Vòng Xoáy Mana · iPhone · coming soon

A two-thumb survivors arena in landscape. Steer with your left thumb while orbiting blades cut through the horde, pick up mana crystals to power a shared mana bar, and cast skills with your right thumb — mark enemies then detonate them, freeze them then shatter them. Bring coins and gear home to grow stronger every run.

Free to play, works offline. Optional reward videos; purchases are never required.
