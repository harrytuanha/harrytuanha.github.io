Privacy Policy

Applies to all games published by 1PGames (Ha Van Tuan, “we”). Last updated: 6 November 2026. Tiếng Việt bên dưới.

1. What we collect

We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Game progress (currency, upgrades, items, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers.

2. Third-party services used inside our games

Some games use the services below. Each service processes data under its own privacy policy. The table at the end lists which game uses which service.

3. Tracking and consent

On iOS we ask permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is used. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google’s consent form before personalised ads; you can change your choice at any time in the game’s Settings → Privacy choices. You can also deny tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights and deleting data

Because progress is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset save data in the game, or by deleting the app. For data held by the services above, use their controls or contact us and we will forward your request.

6. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
